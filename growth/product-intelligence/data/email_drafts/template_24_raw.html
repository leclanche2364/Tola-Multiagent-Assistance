Most nurses tell us the same thing about revalidation: the work isn’t the problem. Asking is.

You need five pieces of practice-related feedback every three years, which can mean five separate occasions of catching someone at the end of a shift and asking them to write something about your practice.

New

Ask for feedback without the awkward ask.

Open the Add Feedback page in Revalidation Copilot and tap Ask for feedback. We’ll create a link you can send using WhatsApp, Teams, text or email.

They open the link, write a few sentences, and the feedback comes back into your app.

For them, it takes about two minutes. There’s no account to create, no app to download and no sign-up.

You can create as many feedback links as you need and revoke any of them at any time.

Feedback can come from more people than you might think.

It doesn’t have to come from another nurse.

It could come from your manager, a doctor or therapist you work with, a student you’ve supervised, or from people you’ve cared for and their families.

The form adapts to whoever is answering, so a patient isn’t asked to comment on your “practice” — they’re asked about their experience of the care you provided.

You stay in control.

Every response arrives in a review list first. You read it and decide whether it should become part of your portfolio.

Nothing is added automatically, and we check feedback for information that could identify a patient before it is saved to your portfolio.

Worth knowing

Feedback collected this way is written directly by the person providing it and is recorded with its submission date, giving you a clearer record of where your feedback came from if you ever need to revisit it later.

Open Revalidation Copilot, go to Add Feedback and tap Ask for feedback to create your first link.
